Forrence & McHugh

Connemara, Mayo, Kerry and Dublin; the Yorkshire mills; the Palatinate; the Suwałki villages; then Youngstown, Wilkes-Barre, Philadelphia and Wilmington. Every claim on this page links to its document, and two independent AI reviewers rated how sure we can be.

The pedigree

Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, click anyone to open their file. The bar under each card is the reviewers' confidence that this person is really your ancestor: the dot is the consensus estimate and the band is the range they gave. Dashed cards are family tradition only.

Fan chart

You at the center, your father's side on the left and your mother's on the right, going back six generations. Paler segments are less certain, and empty segments are ancestors not found yet.

The chronicle

Every dated event in the tree, woven together with the record behind it. The father's side runs down the left and the mother's side down the right, until the two streams meet. Click a document to read it, or a name to open that person's file. Alt-click a family line to show only that line.

Lifelines

Each bar is a life, and each dot is a documented event: hover for details, click to open the person. The shaded bands show what was happening in the world.

Confidence lab

Two independent AI reviewers, Fable 5.2 and Opus 5.5, were told to be hostile to the evidence. Each one re-pulled the records, rated every claim with a confidence estimate and range, and listed what would change its mind. They re-rated as new records came in, over four rounds, and each claim's consensus uses each reviewer's latest rating. Click a claim to see their arguments.
